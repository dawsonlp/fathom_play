# People Dynamics

## Goal

Identify collaboration patterns, tension, disagreement, alignment, and participant approaches using careful language.

## Best Initial Bet

Constrain the model to observable interaction patterns. This is one of the highest-risk targets because unsupported interpersonal analysis can be misleading. It should prefer "no strong finding" over a dramatic but weak claim.

## Strengths To Look For

- Uses qualified language.
- Cites specific interaction moments.
- Separates disagreement about work from interpersonal conflict.

## Weaknesses To Watch

- Infers personality or motive.
- Overstates normal technical disagreement.
- Produces generic "collaborative" comments with no signal.
