# Decisions

## Goal

Extract decisions made, confirmed, or explicitly deferred during the meeting.

## Best Initial Bet

Require the model to distinguish decisions from proposals. This target should be conservative: no decision without evidence that participants accepted, chose, confirmed, or deferred something.

## Strengths To Look For

- Separates decisions from ideas.
- Captures caveats and conditions.
- Identifies deferrals as deferrals rather than decisions.

## Weaknesses To Watch

- Converts a suggested plan into an accepted decision.
- Omits uncertainty.
- Reports broad strategy as if it was decided in the meeting.
