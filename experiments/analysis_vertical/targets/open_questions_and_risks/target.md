# Open Questions And Risks

## Goal

Identify unresolved questions, blockers, risks, assumptions, and dependencies.

## Best Initial Bet

Ask for uncertainty only, not general project concerns. This target may work well with a local model because risks often have explicit language, but it may produce generic filler if the prompt is too open.

## Strengths To Look For

- Finds unresolved items that affect follow-up.
- Preserves the exact uncertainty.
- Avoids generic risk-register language.

## Weaknesses To Watch

- Invents risks that are common to projects but not present in the transcript.
- Overstates severity.
- Turns ordinary complexity into conflict or blocker language.
