# Working Styles

## Goal

Identify participant working-style signals that are visible in the transcript.

## Best Initial Bet

Ask for tentative observations only when there is strong or repeated evidence. This target should not perform personality typing. It should produce caveated, behavior-level observations.

## Strengths To Look For

- Identifies preferences or approaches grounded in transcript behavior.
- Uses caveats.
- Separates one-off utterances from stronger repeated signals.

## Weaknesses To Watch

- Performs personality typing.
- Infers motives.
- Overuses labels like "detail-oriented" without evidence.
