# Significant Ideas

## Goal

Identify important ideas, intentions, principles, proposals, and strategic directions that the meeting is addressing.

This target is not about requested actions. It should capture what the conversation is trying to work out.

## Best Initial Bet

Use short transcript slices and extract idea evidence candidates first. The local model performed better when asked for evidence candidates over small windows, and this target should be less brittle than action-item classification.

## Strengths To Look For

- Finds meaningful ideas rather than generic topic labels.
- Preserves exact evidence.
- Captures intentions such as "we need to make X possible" or "the system should support Y".
- Separates ideas from final decisions or action items.

## Weaknesses To Watch

- Produces broad summaries instead of specific ideas.
- Treats every topic as significant.
- Overstates tentative proposals as settled strategy.
